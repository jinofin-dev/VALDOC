Check Extension of Approval documents before they reach the officer

VALDOC detects edited certificates with Error Level Analysis (ELA), reads key figures with OCR, and compares them against editable AICTE handbook norms. Every finding is advisory—the AICTE officer makes the final decision.

What the pre-check does

Tamper & QR detection

Error Level Analysis (OpenCV + PyMuPDF) highlights dates, stamps, and signatures edited after issue, while ImageHash and Pyzbar check QR codes and reused scans.

Figure extraction & rules

Vision AI and EasyOCR read land area, faculty strength, validity dates, and deposit amounts, comparing each figure with what the college declared and AICTE rules.

Officer & Admin in control

Unclear scans are flagged for manual review (with up to 3 re-uploads). Admins configure AICTE rules in JSON, and officers verify, query, or reject with an audit note.

How an application moves

  1. College uploads

    Fire safety, land and building, faculty, and financial documents are submitted together (max 3 attempts).

  2. AI pre-check runs

    Clarity check, ELA forensics, OCR extraction, and AICTE rule validation finish in seconds.

  3. Officer reviews

    Flagged and unclear items open with highlighted source evidence alongside extracted figures.

  4. Decision is logged

    The college sees the outcome and officer note, while the decision is recorded in the audit log.
